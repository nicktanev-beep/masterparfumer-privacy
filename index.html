Master Parfumer — Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy for Master Parfumer

Last updated: September 30, 2026

Master Parfumer ("the App," "we," "us," or "our") is a desktop
application for perfume formulation, material management, and
project organization. This Privacy Policy explains what data the
App processes, where it is stored, and how it is handled.

By installing or using Master Parfumer, you agree to the practices
described in this Privacy Policy.

1. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

Master Parfumer is designed to work primarily offline and does not
require you to create an account. We do not collect, transmit, or
have access to any of the personal or business data you enter into
the App.

Specifically:

- All data you create in the App — including formulas, raw material
  records, projects, journal notes, inventory entries, and settings
  — is stored locally on your own device.

- We do not operate a server that receives, stores, or processes
  your data.

- We do not collect analytics, telemetry, crash reports, or usage
  statistics.

- We do not use cookies, advertising identifiers, or any tracking
  technologies.

- We do not collect your name, email address, location, or any
  other personally identifiable information.

2. WHERE YOUR DATA IS STORED

On Windows, your App data is stored in your user profile folder:

  %APPDATA%\masterparfumer\master_parfumer_db.json

This file remains entirely under your control. You can back it up,
copy it, edit it, or delete it at any time.

When you uninstall the App, this data file may be removed by
Windows. We strongly recommend that you use the built-in
"Backup DB" feature before uninstalling to preserve your data.

3. NETWORK CONNECTIONS AND THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

The App makes one type of outbound network request: when you use
the "Material Research" feature, the App queries PubChem, a public
chemical database operated by the U.S. National Library of
Medicine (part of the National Institutes of Health).

- The request contains only the search term you enter
  (for example, a material name or CAS number).
- The request is sent directly from your device to PubChem's servers.
- No user data, App data, or identifiers are sent to PubChem.
- PubChem's own privacy practices are governed by the U.S. National
  Institutes of Health privacy policy, available at:
  https://www.nih.gov/web-policies-notices

Aside from PubChem lookups, the App does not communicate with any
external server.

4. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

The App is a professional tool intended for adult users and is not
directed at children under the age of 13. Because the App does not
collect any personal information, it does not knowingly collect
information from children.

5. DATA SECURITY

Because all App data is stored locally on your device, its security
depends on the security of your device. We recommend using a
password-protected Windows account and keeping regular backups of
your App data.

6. YOUR RIGHTS AND CONTROL

You have full control over your data at all times:

- You can access it directly in the App.
- You can export it using the "Backup DB" feature.
- You can restore it using the "Restore DB" feature.
- You can delete it by uninstalling the App or by deleting the data
  file directly.

Because we do not collect any data, there is nothing for us to
disclose, correct, or delete on your behalf.

7. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes
will be reflected in the "Last updated" date at the top of this
document. Continued use of the App after changes are posted
constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
